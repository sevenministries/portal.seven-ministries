How Enrollment Works

Fees open the door. The portal does the rest.

Every credential issued through the registrar's office is tied to a real learner and a settled account — no portal ID exists without it.

1

Fees are settled

A guardian or student completes payment at the registrar's office for the current term.

2

A portal ID is issued

The registrar generates a unique student ID and password, tied to the student's class and subjects.

3

Learning begins

The student signs in to view lessons, join live classes, and submit assignments to their teachers.
