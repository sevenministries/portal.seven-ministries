How Enrollment Works

Fees open the door. The portal does the rest.

Every credential issued through the registrar's office is tied to a real learner and a settled account — no portal ID exists without it.

1

Fees are settled

A guardian or student completes payment at the registrar's office for the current term.

2

A portal ID is issued

The registrar generates a unique student ID and password, tied to the student's class and subjects.

3

Learning begins

The student signs in to view lessons, join live classes, and submit assignments to their teachers.

Three Portals, One Institution

Built around who uses it.

Every role sees exactly what they need — nothing borrowed from a generic school template.

Students

View lesson plans, join live online classes, submit assignments and track grades — all under one portal ID.

Teachers

Deliver online lessons, build lesson plans, set assignments, and grade student submissions by subject and class.

Registrar

Record fee payments, issue student and teacher portal IDs, and keep the institution's roll up to date.
